PSIR 172 – MUN Simulation Practice App

This app lets you rehearse the Model UN experience before the live class simulation. It is a practice committee: you choose your delegation, select a mode, respond to prompts, and receive coaching-style feedback.

Use it individually or with your delegation partner to practice opening speeches, moderated debate, caucusing, and resolution drafting.

How this practice app works

  • Opening Speech mode helps you practice your first formal intervention.
  • Moderated Debate mode gives you chair prompts and simulated challenges.
  • Caucus mode helps you test negotiation and alliance language.
  • Clause Drafting mode helps you practice preambulatory and operative language.
  • Full Mini-Sim mode walks you through several stages in sequence.
