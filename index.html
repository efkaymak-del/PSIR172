Paragraph Structure (PEEL)

Point — State your topic sentence clearly
Evidence — Provide data, quotes, or examples
Explanation — Analyse what the evidence means
Link — Connect back to your thesis

Transition Phrases

Adding: Furthermore, Moreover, In addition
Contrasting: However, Nevertheless, Conversely
Cause/Effect: Consequently, Therefore, As a result
Exemplifying: For instance, Specifically, To illustrate
Concluding: In summary, Ultimately, This demonstrates

MUN Vocabulary

Preambular: Noting, Bearing in mind, Deeply concerned, Recalling, Guided by
Operative: Calls upon, Urges, Encourages, Requests, Decides, Recommends, Condemns
Procedural: Motion to table, Right of reply, Point of order, Moderated caucus, Unmoderated caucus

Speech Template

1. Address — "Honourable Chair, Distinguished Delegates"
2. Stake — Why this matters to your country
3. Position — Your stance in 1–2 sentences
4. Evidence — One concrete fact or example
5. Proposal — What you want the committee to do
6. Close — Call for cooperation / Thank the chair

Campaign Guide

Message → Audience → Goal → Medium → Tool.
Never start with "I want to make a poster." Start with "I want to change how people think about X."

Tool Recommendations

Canva

Best for: posters, social media, flyers, slides
  • One dominant element + clear headline
  • 5-second readability test
  • Colours match tone (warm = urgency, cool = trust)
  • One clear call to action

AI-Assisted Video

Best for: scripts, subtitles, voiceovers, storyboarding
  • Write full script first
  • First 3 seconds = hook
  • Always add captions
  • Verify every claim independently
