PSIR 172 — Midterm Study Guide

Spring 2026 · Covers Weeks 1–7 · Exam: 20 marks, 80 minutes

📋 Exam Format

The midterm is closed book. No notes, no devices. Duration: 80 minutes. Total: 20 marks (= 20% of your final grade).

Section A — Multiple Choice (8 marks)
8 questions, 1 mark each. Four options. Covers all topics from Weeks 1–7.
Section B — Short Analysis (4 marks)
Read a short passage (a campaign message, press statement, or advertisement). Two questions: identify the framing and identify the persuasion mode. Write 3–5 sentences for each answer.
Section C — Applied Scenario (8 marks)
You are given a realistic scenario and write a campaign communication plan (300–400 words). Four parts worth 2 marks each:
(a) Target audience — who and why
(b) Framing strategy — positive, negative, or both, with example
(c) Key message — 3–4 sentences using at least 2 persuasion modes
(d) Ethical awareness — one risk and how to avoid it

💡 How to Prepare

1. Review the concepts in this guide. Can you explain each term in your own words?

2. Practise Section C. Pick any issue and write a 300-word campaign plan. Do it twice.

3. Know the differences: research question vs. recommendation; positive vs. negative framing; ethos vs. pathos vs. logos; correlation vs. causation; policy brief vs. essay; paid vs. earned media.

4. Don't memorise McNair. Understand the ideas — professionalisation, message discipline, negative campaigning.

5. Read exam questions carefully. In Section C, marks are given for each part (a–d). Don't skip any.
